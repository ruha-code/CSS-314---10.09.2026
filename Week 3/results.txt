Questions

1. Look at your row for 16 threads. Why didn't your 8-core CPU
   run twice as fast as 8 threads?

   Because the CPU has 8 physical cores and 16 SMT threads.
   At T=8 all physical cores are already busy. Extra SMT
   threads share the same ALUs / FPU / L1-L2 caches, so going
   from 8 to 16 only gives 4.34x -> 5.97x instead of 2x.
   At T=32 context switching makes it even slower.

2. Why was the synchronized version in Part 2 slower than
   running on one single core?

   Every increment grabs a lock. Cores spend most of their
   time waiting on the lock and bouncing the cache line
   instead of doing math. Single thread has no lock overhead
   so it is ~38x faster.
